# BeautyCraft

**A visual-first Fabric modpack for players who want Minecraft to look as good as it plays.**

BeautyCraft is built around one goal: make the vanilla world genuinely beautiful, without turning it into a different game. Sunsets actually feel dramatic, forests feel alive, seasons change, and villages feel lived-in — all while running smoothly thanks to a solid performance backbone.

## What's inside

- **116 mods**, **24 curated resource packs**, and a massive **228 shaderpacks** bundled in, so you can find the exact look you want without hunting Modrinth yourself.
- Built on **Fabric Loader 0.19.3** for Minecraft **1.26.2**.

## Performance first

Beauty shouldn't cost you your frame rate. BeautyCraft leans on **Sodium**, **Lithium**, **Iris**, **Voxy**, and **Distant Horizons** to keep the world rendering fast and far, even with shaders and extra visual mods running.

## Atmosphere & immersion

- **Serene Seasons** brings real seasonal change to the world.
- **AmbientSounds**, **Sound Physics Remastered**, and **Presence Footsteps** layer in a living soundscape.
- **Dynamic Lights**, **Coolrain**, **Blur+**, and **Subtle/Particle Effects** add atmospheric polish to weather, lighting, and movement.
- **Dramatic Skys**, **Fresh Animations**, **Better Leaves**, **Round Trees**, and dozens more resource packs reshape how the world looks and moves.

## Building & decoration

An enormous decoration toolkit for builders: the full **Macaw's** furniture, bridges, windows, fences, roofs, and stairs lineup, plus **Waystones**, **Trading Post**, **Nether Chested**, **Double Doors**, **Stoneworks**, and structure packs like **Moog's Voyager Structures** and **Towns and Towers** to fill your world with detail.

## Character & animation

**Not Enough Animations**, **3D Skin Layers**, **Player Animation Lib**, **Emotecraft**, **Chat Heads**, and **Do a Barrel Roll** give players and mobs far more life and expression than vanilla.

## Quality of life

**JEI**, **Mod Menu**, **Cloth Config**, **YACL**, **Inventory Profiles Next**, **Just Zoom**, **Resourcify**, **Controlify**, and **Nature's Compass** round things out so the pack is as convenient to play as it is nice to look at.

## Multiplayer ready

**LuckPerms**, **AuthMe**, **Simple Voice Chat**, and **ViaFabric** are included for server owners who want a polished, sociable community pack.

## Requirements

- Minecraft **1.26.2**
- **Fabric Loader 0.19.3**
- A GPU capable of running Iris shaders is recommended if you plan to use the included shaderpacks.

## Notes

This pack ships a large library of shaderpacks and resource packs so you can experiment freely — feel free to disable anything you don't use to save disk space and load times. All credit for individual mods, resource packs, and shaders goes to their respective authors on Modrinth.
